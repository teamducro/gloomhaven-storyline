# Gloomhaven 2nd Edition (gh2)

Background, sources and decisions: `docs/gh2e/notes/README.md` and `docs/gh2e/notes/plans/`.

## Wiring

- Chosen per campaign in Settings > Enabled Games (`GamesEnabled.vue`): stored as `edition: 2` on the gh `sheet`, read by `SheetRepository.gloomhavenGame()`. gh and gh2 share one slot in the game switch; both keep their own data. Covered by the GH licence (`GameData.includedIn()`, API receives `accessGame = 'gh'`).
- Generated by scripts outside the repo: `games/gh2.json` (scenarios, achievements, characters, items, `events`), the gh2 lang blocks, map sticker positions, `svg/storylines/gh2.svg` and the event card images.

## Campaign sheet (`pages/PartyGh2.vue`, model `Sheet`)

Labelled "Campaign sheet" but stored as a plain `Sheet` (`sheet-gh2`).

- Faction reputation: `sheet.factionReputation` with keys `demons`, `merchant-guild`, `military`, range -10..12 (cap 20 once sheet unlock 17/18/19 is ticked). No party reputation; shop price modifier is 0.
- Reputation thresholds are stored as `Party` achievements `P2RD6/12`, `P2RME6/12`, `P2RMI6/12`, `P2R16`, `P2R20`, auto-gained (gain only) by `PartyGh2`. First faction at 13 ticks sheet unlock 0 and adds City event 57 (data in `sections` of `games/gh2.json`, with reputation 9 events, prosperity events and first-retirement unlock 7).
- Inspiration (number), imbuement (80 boxes; every 5th = +1 prosperity, every 10th = section), prosperity (sections at boxes 13/23/34/46), donations (+1 prosperity per 50 gold, max 20).
- 20 sheet unlocks: index order is persisted, append only.
- Decks: City/Road start 1–20 (max C100 / R77). Unlocking a class adds its class events (`classEvents`); retiring reads the mat's section, no events. Event cards have a front and one back image; `events` maps each outcome (A–D) to where it starts on the back, as % of its height (e.g. `C-2 {A:0, B:44.9, C:84.6}`); outcomes not chosen are blurred (`EventCard.vue blurredSections`).

## Scenarios

- Roots 0 (Training Course) and 1. Variants 23A/23B/25A/25B are ids 101–104 with a display `number`.
- Conclusion decisions are data-driven: `prompt: "gh2-<id>"` + `prompt_unlocks[n-1]`.
- `required_by` mixes scenario ints and achievement ids. No regions, pages or story summaries. Solo 201–218 (chapter 100).
- `ScenarioCompletedService` is off.

## Achievements

`achievements` in `games/gh2.json`: Global `G2*`, city rule group `CR2` (start `G2AUP` An Uneasy Peace, then `G2CRD` Demons, `G2CRME` Merchants, `G2CRMI` Military). No stacking stickers. `G2TPE` enables enhancements.

## Characters and items

- Classes have ids suffixed `2` (`BR2` Bruiser, `SC2` Silent Knife, `ST2` Soultether from Summoner, `WF2` Wildfury from Beast Tyrant). Masteries; perks of `type: "custom"` have no `cards` (text only). Only the chosen edition's classes are playable, in every game; unlocks of hidden classes are kept.
- Personal quests 1–22 unlock classes and add class events.
- Shop by prosperity (thresholds over items 1–68, plus 116), item designs 69–170, flip items (`backDesc`).
- Enhancements: GH2 cost table (FH costs without Regenerate), no limit on enhanced cards. Difficulty table adds hazardous terrain.

## Not modelled (deferred in the plans)

Item faction-reputation requirements, personal quest deck gating, "all parents required" multi-parent unlocks, Challenger's Rift, cross-game items, countable achievements.
