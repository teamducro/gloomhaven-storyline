# Gloomhaven Storyline

Campaign tracker for the Gloomhaven family of board games. Terms follow each game's rulebook; tracker-only words are marked as such. Short forms in parentheses: GH Gloomhaven, FC Forgotten Circles, GH2 Gloomhaven 2nd Edition, FH Frosthaven, CS Crimson Scales.

## Language

### Games

**Game**: a standalone product with its own campaign: Gloomhaven, Gloomhaven 2nd Edition, Frosthaven, Jaws of the Lion, Crimson Scales.
_Avoid_: edition, box

**Gloomhaven 2nd Edition**: the revised Gloomhaven, a separate game (own scenarios, classes, faction reputation, imbuement). In progress.

**Expansion**: content continuing one game's campaign. Forgotten Circles is an expansion of Gloomhaven (scenarios, Diviner, Rift events), viewable with its own storyline.
_Avoid_: calling Forgotten Circles a game

**Crossover pack**: content added to any game. The Mercenary Pack (classes, solo scenarios, items).
_Avoid_: expansion, game

### Campaigns and sheets

**Campaign**: one group's playthrough, holding progress for every game it plays.
_Avoid_: story, save

**Local campaign**: kept on one device only. **Shared campaign**: synced to the server, joined with a **campaign code** (short-lived, optionally read-only).
_Avoid_: story, story code, cloud/pro campaign

**Party sheet**: town and party progress in Gloomhaven (+ Forgotten Circles), Jaws of the Lion, Crimson Scales.
**Campaign sheet**: its replacement in Frosthaven and Gloomhaven 2nd Edition.
_Avoid_: "sheet" alone

**Party**: the characters currently playing together.
_Avoid_: using it for the party sheet

**Sheet unlock**: a reward box on the party or campaign sheet, ticked when its condition is met (e.g. "five Ancient Technology: open Envelope A"; Crimson Scales "every five completed scenarios").
_Avoid_: unlock (as noun), milestone, envelope trigger

**Unlock**: verb only; a scenario, class, building or item becomes available.

**Section**: a numbered section-book entry to read.

**Envelope**: sealed content opened when a sheet unlock, puzzle or achievement says so (Envelope A, X).

### Scenarios

**Scenario**: one playable mission, numbered per game.

**Scenario state** (tracker words): **hidden** not yet unlocked; **incomplete** unlocked and playable; **complete** won; **blocked** excluded by another outcome; **required** unlocked but requirements unmet.
_Avoid_: locked, available, locked out, unplayable

**Requirement**: a condition to play a scenario: scenarios complete/incomplete, an achievement held or not, a vehicle built, a calendar week reached, a puzzle solved.
_Avoid_: prerequisite

**Side scenario**: outside the main storyline, usually unlocked by an event, achievement or personal quest.
**Solo scenario**: for one class, played alone.
**Coupled scenarios**: two scenarios sharing one map location.

**Chapter**: a storyline group of scenarios (story arcs, plus side groups like solo, personal quests, events).
**Region**: an area of the Gloomhaven world map.

**Branch choice**: picking which linked scenario to unlock after a completion.
_Avoid_: choice (alone), prompt

**Conclusion decision**: a numbered decision in a conclusion or section whose options give different rewards, achievements or unlocks.
_Avoid_: prompt, prompt choice

**Reward**: what a conclusion grants (gold, XP, prosperity, items, achievements, unlocks).
**Treasure**: a numbered tile looted in a scenario; may unlock scenarios, achievements or item designs.

**Story summary** (tracker): recap of a storyline arc so far.
_Avoid_: quest, preceding events

**Storyline**: the flowchart of a game's scenarios and how they unlock each other.

### Achievements

**Achievement**: a campaign fact (sticker) that scenarios can require, award or remove.
**Global achievement**: about the world (GH, FC, CS, GH2). **Party achievement**: about one party (GH, FC, CS). **Campaign achievement**: Frosthaven campaign sheet sticker.
**City rule**: exclusive global achievements; gaining one replaces the current.
**Lost**: removed by a scenario outcome, as opposed to never gained.
_Avoid_: removed
**Manual achievement**: gained outside scenarios (e.g. event card), recorded by hand.

### Town and party

**Reputation**: party standing, -20..20, changes shop prices (GH, FC, CS).
**Prosperity**: town level 1–9; in most games sets the shop. **Prosperity checkmark**: one box on its track.
_Avoid_: prosperity index
**Donation**: gold given to the Sanctuary of the Great Oak; can raise prosperity.
_Avoid_: support for the app developer
**Event deck**: cards read between scenarios: City, Road, Rift (FC), Boat (FH), FH Summer/Winter Outpost and Road. **Draw**: take one at random.
**Scenario level**: the difficulty played at, from character levels plus adjustment.
_Avoid_: difficulty

### Gloomhaven 2nd Edition

**Faction reputation**: three tracks (Demons, Merchant's Guild, Military) replacing reputation.
_Avoid_: merchants, merchant-guild, Shields, The Sect
**Reputation threshold**: a faction level (6, 12, 16, 20) scenarios can require.
_Avoid_: party/reputation achievement
**Imbuement**: sheet track; every 5th box +1 prosperity, every 10th a section.
**Inspiration**: party resource gained by finishing scenarios with fewer than four characters, spent on character advancement (also FH).
**Class event**: event card added when its class is unlocked.

### Frosthaven

**Outpost**: the Frosthaven settlement built up between scenarios.
_Avoid_: town, city
**Calendar**: passed weeks, alternating ten summer and ten winter; some weeks carry sections. **Season**: summer or winter, picks the event decks.
**Building**: numbered outpost structure with levels. **Build**: available → level 1. **Upgrade**: +1 level. **Damaged**: harmed, still working, repaired with any materials. **Wrecked**: destroyed by an attack, effects lost. **Rebuild**: restore a wrecked building.
_Avoid_: destroyed, repair (for wrecked)
**Resources**: **materials** (lumber, metal, hide) and **herbs** (arrowvine, axenut, corpsecap, flamefruit, rockroot, snowthistle), held by outpost or character.
**Morale**: 0–20, sets defense. **Defense**: protection from attacks (morale + walls).
**Soldier**: Barracks guard. **Town guard**: the defenders, with their own attack modifier deck. **Town guard checkmark** earns **town guard perks** (deck changes).
**Wall**: adds defense. **Town feature**: Well, Statue, Stage, Ice Spike, Portcullis.
**Workshop vehicle**: Boat, Sled, Climbing Gear; scenarios can require them.
_Avoid_: rope, overlay
**Map sticker**: anything stuck on a map (locations, buildings, vehicles, achievements).
_Avoid_: overlay
**Alchemy chart**: Alchemist herb combinations. **Brew**: combine herbs into a potion.
_Avoid_: unlock (for brew)
**Puzzle**: a requirement solved via the puzzle book.
**Mastery**: class challenge that earns a perk (also GH2, Mercenary Pack).

### Crimson Scales

**Alliance**: party achievement (Oak or Inox Alliance) chosen via a city event; decides which side scenarios unlock.
**Milestone card**: an ability card a class unlocks by reaching a milestone.

### Jaws of the Lion

**Tutorial scenarios**: scenarios 1–5, teaching the rules with tutorial ability cards.

### Characters

**Class**: a playable character type. **Starting class**: available from the start. **Class unlock**: a locked class becoming playable.
_Avoid_: hero, profession
**Character**: one player's instance of a class (level, XP, gold, items, perks).
**Personal quest**: secret retirement goal; completing it retires the character and unlocks a class, scenario or building.
_Avoid_: quest (alone)
**Retire**: leave the party after the personal quest. **Remove**: delete permanently (retiring does this in a local campaign).
_Avoid_: archive
**Perk**: permanent attack modifier deck change. **Perk checkmark**: from battle goals, three give a perk.
_Avoid_: check
**Ability card**: card from a class deck with level and initiative. **Attack modifier deck**: drawn on each attack. **Enhancement**: sticker improving an ability card.

### Items

**Item**: equipment, numbered per game. **Shop**: items buyable now, set by prosperity, buildings or scenarios.
**Item design**: item made available outside shop rules (reward, treasure).
_Aliases_: blueprint (Frosthaven)
**Craft**: make a Frosthaven item from resources (and items) instead of gold.
**Cross-game item**: item from another game enabled in this campaign.
